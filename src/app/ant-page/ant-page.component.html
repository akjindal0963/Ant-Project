WHO’S MAKING THEMSELVES AT HOME?

Meet the usual suspects.

Melbourne homes commonly see a few different ant species. Correct identification helps us choose the right approach.

01 / INDOORS

Black house ants

Often follow food and water into kitchens, pantries and bathrooms.

02 / GARDENS

Coastal brown ants

Common in lawns and garden beds, sometimes nesting near paving.

03 / TRAILS

Argentine ants

Can form connected colonies and persistent trails around buildings.
